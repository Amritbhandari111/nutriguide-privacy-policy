Data Collection and Usage

The Application collects minimal information.

The following information may be collected or processed by third-party services used in the Application:

  • Advertising ID: Google AdMob may collect or process the Advertising ID to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, and provide related advertising services.

We do not directly collect:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers or contact information
  • Precise location data
  • User account information
  • Device identifiers other than information that may be processed by third-party advertising services such as AdMob
  • App usage or analytics data directly by us

Information that you enter into the Application, such as calorie records, food information, BMI information, meal information, reminder settings, and similar information, remains stored locally on your Device unless a particular feature requires communication with an external service.

We do not operate a user account system or our own server/database for storing your personal calorie, BMI, meal, or reminder information.

Local Data Storage

The Application may store information locally on your Device to provide features such as:

  • Calorie tracking
  • Food tracking
  • BMI calculations or records
  • Meal information
  • Reminder settings
  • App preferences

This information is stored locally on your Device and is not intentionally uploaded to servers operated by us.

Images or camera frames used for barcode scanning are processed for barcode recognition and are not intentionally stored by us.

Data Retention

We do not retain your calorie records, BMI records, meal information, reminder information, barcode images, or similar user-entered personal information on servers operated by us because we do not operate a server-side database for this information.

Information stored locally by the Application remains on your Device until one of the following occurs:

  • You delete the relevant information within the Application, where a deletion option is available.
  • You clear the Application's storage or data through your Device settings.
  • You uninstall the Application.

After the Application's data is cleared or the Application is uninstalled, locally stored information may be deleted according to your Device's operating system and backup settings.

Information collected or processed independently by third-party services such as Google AdMob may be retained according to the respective third party's own data retention policies. We do not control the retention periods applied by those third-party providers.

Data Deletion

Because we do not maintain user accounts or store your calorie, BMI, meal, reminder, or other locally entered personal information on our own servers, there is generally no server-side personal app data maintained by us that requires deletion.

You can delete locally stored Application data using one or more of the following methods:

  1. Delete information within the Application where a deletion option is available.
  2. Clear the Application's data through your Android Device settings. This can generally be done by going to:

    Settings > Apps > Calorie Tracker, Recipes & BMI > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Storage

    The exact menu names may vary depending on your Device.
  3. Uninstall the Application from your Device.

Information collected or processed independently by third-party services, including Google AdMob, is subject to those providers' own privacy, retention, and deletion policies.

Permissions Used

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS – Allows the Application to send reminders, meal notifications, and other requested notifications.
  • VIBRATE – Enables vibration for reminders and notifications.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID – Allows supported advertising services such as Google AdMob to access the Advertising ID where permitted.
  • WAKE_LOCK – Helps notifications and scheduled tasks operate properly.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED – Allows alarms and reminders to be rescheduled after the Device restarts.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM – Allows the Application to schedule meal and health reminders at specific times where supported.
  • REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS – May be used to help scheduled reminders operate reliably when battery optimization restrictions are enabled.
  • CAMERA – Used for barcode scanning to help users add foods. Images used for barcode scanning are not intentionally stored or shared by us.
  • INTERNET – Required for displaying advertisements and retrieving recipe or nutrition information from external services or APIs.
